## 📂 What's Inside

Before starting the classes, it is really important to have a cluster running on your machine:

- [**Getting Started**](../00-getting-started/lab-notes.md) — Initial setup and learning environment

Topics of the study group:

- [**Introducing the Certified Kubernetes Administrator**](01-introducing-cka/index.md) - What the exam contains, Kubernetes architecture
- [**Kubernetes Cluster**](02-kubernetes-cluster/index.md) - Cluster components
- [**Identity and Access Management**](03-identity-access-management/index.md) - Access control, users, groups, service accounts
- [**Deploying Applications in Kubernetes**](04-deploying-applications/index.md) - Scheduling applications and configuring pods
- [**Running applications in Kubernetes**](05-running-applications/index.md) - Orchestrating applications
- [**Communication in a Kubernetes Cluster**](06-communication/index.md) - DNS, Ingress and ingress controllers, networking
- [**Storing in Kubernetes**](07-storage/index.md) - Persistent volumes, PVC, Storage class
- [**Troubleshooting Kubernetes**](08-troubleshooting/index.md) - Understanding logs, component failures
- [**Taking the test**](09-taking-the-test/index.md) - We can do it, keep working, fail, try again, and then succeed

- **Weekly Reflections** — Document personal takeaways, challenges, and growth areas. It is really important to document everything you are learning, no matter if it looks simple: if you study something and write it in your own words, the knowledge stays in your brain more easily. Explain and share with the community what you did, how you did it and what you are learning; you never know when you can help someone find their own way.

## Q&A

- What is [**CNCF**](https://www.cncf.io/)? CNCF is the open source, vendor-neutral hub of cloud native computing, hosting projects like Kubernetes and Prometheus to make cloud native universal and sustainable.

- Where can I find the [**Kubernetes documentation**](https://kubernetes.io/docs/home/)?

- What is [**KubeSkills**](https://community.kubeskills.com/feed)? KubeSkills is a Kubernetes community where you can talk with professionals in the industry who operate real clusters every day, and do guided projects, labs, tech discussions and many exercises.

- What is [**GROW Portfolio**](https://kubeskills.com/grow/)? GROW is where your Kubernetes journey turns into a living, breathing portfolio of YAML-powered awesomeness.

- What kind of [**CNCF certifications**](https://www.cncf.io/training/certification/) exist?
