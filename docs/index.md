## 🧠 Why this way of learning?

It is very important to build a portfolio that shows your skills and the way you are learning. Try to be 1% better every single day, and build a strong mindset based on discipline, consistency, studying and documenting everything.

The best way to learn is failing: when you fail, you must be patient with yourself and give your brain time to understand hard topics. Work on handling the frustration of not understanding things at first; if you try many times, things start to flow. Practice and exercise your brain muscles by repetition.
